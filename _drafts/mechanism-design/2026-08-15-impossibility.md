## Decisive coalitions

<!-- Define: a coalition is decisive if the group ranking follows it whenever it agrees.
     Show that under IIA + unanimity the decisive sets are closed under intersection,
     supersets, and complementation-or-not. -->

## That is an ultrafilter

<!-- Kirman & Sondermann (1972). The decisive coalitions form an ultrafilter on the
     set of voters. On a finite set every ultrafilter is principal. A principal
     ultrafilter is generated by a single element. That element is the dictator.

     The theorem then reads: an Arrovian welfare function *is* an ultrafilter, and
     finiteness is what makes ultrafilters boring. -->

## The infinite case, as a sanity check

<!-- With infinitely many voters, non-principal ultrafilters exist, so non-dictatorial
     Arrovian functions exist — but the "dictator" is only invisible, not absent
     (every decisive coalition still shrinks without limit). Good for showing which
     axiom was doing the work. -->
