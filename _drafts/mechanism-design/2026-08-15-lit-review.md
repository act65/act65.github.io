## If you read four things

1. Börgers, *An Introduction to the Theory of Mechanism Design* (2015) — the short version.
2. Brandt, Conitzer, Endriss, Lang & Procaccia (eds), *Handbook of Computational Social
   Choice* (2016) — the reference, free online.
3. Hartline, *Mechanism Design and Approximation* — free book draft; approximation as a
   design philosophy rather than a compromise.
4. Curry, Fan, Jiang, Ravindranath, Wang & Parkes, "Automated Mechanism Design: A Survey"
   (SIGecom Exchanges, 2025) — where the computational side actually is.
