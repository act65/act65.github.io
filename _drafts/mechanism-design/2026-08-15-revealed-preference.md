## Afriat's theorem

<!-- The result: a finite set of price–quantity observations is rationalisable by a
     continuous, concave, monotone, locally non-satiated utility function iff the
     data satisfy cyclical consistency (GARP).

     Two features to draw out, because both recur everywhere in Arc C:

     1. It is *constructive*. The proof builds a utility function — a piecewise-linear
        one from the Afriat numbers. You do not just learn that an explanation exists.

     2. It is *massively underdetermined*. The constructed function is one of
        infinitely many, and concavity was an assumption that bought tractability
        rather than a fact recovered from the data.

     So the honest summary is: rationalisable almost always, uniquely almost never. -->
