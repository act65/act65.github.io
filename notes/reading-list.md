# Long-form reading — sources to explore

A curated map of long-form journalism, magazines, and personal blogs, tuned to my interests
(math/mechanism reframes, structural history, EA/rationalist, physics, interactive explainers,
market quirks). Organised by how it connects to my existing favourites:
[Our World in Data](https://ourworldindata.org/latest), the [EA Forum](https://ea.greaterwrong.com/),
and [Quanta](https://quantamagazine.org/).

Tick them off as I go. ⭐ = strongest matches to start with.

---

## Start here — six bullseyes

- [ ] ⭐ **[Money Stuff](https://www.bloomberg.com/account/newsletters/money-stuff) — Matt Levine** — daily column on finance's absurdities and market microstructure, genuinely witty. My market-quirks brainstorm in professional form, every day. Free by email.
- [ ] ⭐ **[A Collection of Unmitigated Pedantry (ACOUP)](https://acoup.blog/)** — Bret Devereaux on Roman/military/economic history: mechanism-first, structural, how power and wealth shaped ordinary lives. The closest thing online to how I approach history.
- [ ] ⭐ **[Three-Toed Sloth](http://bactra.org/weblog/) — Cosma Shalizi** — polymath statistician on complexity, inference, and the limits of the knowable. Dense, funny, permanently skeptical.
- [ ] ⭐ **[Asterisk Magazine](https://asteriskmag.com/)** — EA-adjacent quarterly: forecasting, biosecurity, economics, AI, history, in edited prose. The step up from the EA Forum.
- [ ] ⭐ **[Bartosz Ciechanowski](https://ciechanow.ski/)** — interactive explorable essays (GPS, lenses, gears, engines) where you drag the diagram. My hover-gloss/cipher-widget aesthetic at its peak.
- [ ] ⭐ **[Works in Progress](https://worksinprogress.co/)** — progress-studies magazine: science, economics, mechanism design. The OWID sensibility in essay form.

## Deep science & math (extends Quanta)

- [ ] **[Nautilus](https://nautil.us/)** — one big science/philosophy theme per issue, beautifully written.
- [ ] **[Aeon](https://aeon.co/)** + sister site **[Psyche](https://psyche.co/)** — philosophy, science, humanities as serious essays. My "reframe a philosophical idea" territory.
- [ ] **[Asimov Press](https://press.asimov.com/)** — sharp long-form on biology and biotech (immunology, antibiotics, synthetic bio).
- [ ] **[Shtetl-Optimized](https://scottaaronson.blog/) — Scott Aaronson** — quantum computing, complexity, limits of computation. Opinionated and deep.
- [ ] **[Azimuth](https://johncarlosbaez.wordpress.com/) — John Baez** — mathematical physics, category theory, entropy, networks. Unification/isomorphism-hunting.
- [ ] **[argmin](https://www.argmin.net/) — Ben Recht** — optimization, ML, control theory, first-principles and skeptical.

## Interactive / explorable explanations (the JS-demo instinct)

- [ ] **[Explorable Explanations hub](https://explorabl.es/)** + the **[awesome-explorables list](https://github.com/blob42/awesome-explorables)** — gateways to hundreds of interactive essays.
- [ ] **[Nicky Case](https://ncase.me/)** — playable explanations of game theory, networks, social dynamics ("Evolution of Trust" is a mechanism-design gem).
- [ ] **[Red Blob Games](https://www.redblobgames.com/) — Amit Patel** — the definitive interactive explainers of pathfinding and algorithms.
- [ ] **[Seeing Theory](https://seeing-theory.brown.edu/)** — visual, interactive probability and statistics.
- [ ] **[Better Explained](https://betterexplained.com/) — Kalid Azad** — intuition-first math (e, Fourier, Bayes).
- [ ] **[samwho.dev](https://samwho.dev/) — Sam Rose** — recent, gorgeous interactive pieces on algorithms and systems.
- [ ] **[Transformer Circuits](https://transformer-circuits.pub/)** (Anthropic interpretability) + the archived **[Distill](https://distill.pub/)** — interactive ML research.

## Rationalist / EA / forecasting (extends the EA Forum)

- [ ] **[Astral Codex Ten](https://www.astralcodexten.com/) — Scott Alexander** — the continuation of SSC; still the best of the genre.
- [ ] **[Don't Worry About the Vase](https://thezvi.substack.com/) — Zvi Mowshowitz** — exhaustive AI + rationality roundups. The firehose, but authoritative.
- [ ] **[Slime Mold Time Mold](https://slimemoldtimemold.com/)** — wild, rigorous, contrarian investigations (the obesity series is a cult classic).
- [ ] **[Nintil](https://nintil.com/) — José Luis Ricón** — deep, cited deep-dives on science, longevity, progress, metascience.
- [ ] **[Metaculus](https://www.metaculus.com/)** — community forecasting; prediction-markets-for-truth, live.

## Economics, markets & mechanism design

- [ ] **[Marginal Revolution](https://marginalrevolution.com/) — Cowen & Tabarrok** — the daily econ-and-everything blog, plus great "assorted links" for discovery.
- [ ] **[Noahpinion](https://www.noahpinion.blog/) — Noah Smith** — accessible, opinionated macro and development economics.
- [ ] **[Bits about Money](https://www.bitsaboutmoney.com/) — Patrick McKenzie** — how the financial plumbing actually works, mechanism by mechanism.
- [ ] **[interfluidity](https://www.interfluidity.com/) — Steve Randy Waldman** — slow, deep, morally-serious essays on finance and institutions.
- [ ] **[Construction Physics](https://www.construction-physics.com/) — Brian Potter** — why building things is hard, in quantified detail.
- [ ] **[Vitalik Buterin](https://vitalik.eth.limo/)** — mechanism design, crypto-economics, governance from first principles (Rain/DAO interests).

## Structural & economic history (the brutal-history thread)

- [ ] **[Pseudoerasmus](https://pseudoerasmus.com/)** — dense, combative economic history and comparative development.
- [ ] **[Age of Invention](https://www.ageofinvention.xyz/) — Anton Howes** — the history of innovation; why the Industrial Revolution happened where it did.
- [ ] **[The Scholar's Stage](https://scholars-stage.org/) — Tanner Greer** — strategy, comparative history, the long view.
- [ ] **[Broadstreet](https://broadstreet.blog/)** + **[Davis Kedrosky](https://blog.daviskedrosky.com/)** — academic economic history made readable ("how much was stolen / how did growth happen").

## Data, progress & global development (extends OWID)

- [ ] **[The Pudding](https://pudding.cool/)** — data-driven *visual* essays; journalism-as-dataviz.
- [ ] **[Roots of Progress](https://rootsofprogress.org/) — Jason Crawford** — the philosophy and history of technological progress.
- [ ] **[OWID blog & insights](https://ourworldindata.org/blog)** — the written analysis behind the charts I already read.

## AI / alignment / ML (the research world)

- [ ] **[Import AI](https://importai.substack.com/) — Jack Clark** — the weekly signal on AI research and policy.
- [ ] **[Lil'Log](https://lilianweng.github.io/) — Lilian Weng** — the clearest technical deep-dives on modern ML (diffusion, RL, agents).
- [ ] **[Gwern](https://gwern.net/)** ⭐ — the omnivore's omnivore: ML, statistics, genetics, the long tail of everything, quantified and permanent. A whole universe.

## General long-form & slow journalism

- [ ] **[Damn Interesting](https://www.damninteresting.com/)** — meticulously-researched narrative non-fiction on strange true history.
- [ ] **[Longreads](https://longreads.com/)** — aggregator of the best long journalism across the web.
- [ ] **[Delayed Gratification](https://www.slow-journalism.com/)** — "slow journalism" revisiting stories after the news cycle moves on.

## How to keep finding more (the meta-layer)

- [ ] **[The Browser](https://thebrowser.com/)** — Robert Cottrell hand-picks ~5 excellent long reads daily. Best curation service for this taste (paid).
- [ ] **[Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/)** + **[Lobsters](https://lobste.rs/)** — surface deep technical/science posts and personal blogs constantly.
- [ ] **Blogroll-surfing** — every blog above links its own favourites; ACX, Marginal Revolution, and Gwern keep link roundups that are pure discovery fuel.
- [ ] **Get an RSS reader** (Feedly / Inoreader / NetNewsWire) — the way to keep up with 30 personal blogs without a feed algorithm deciding for me.
- [ ] **[Five Books](https://fivebooks.com/)** + r/slatestarcodex — expert reading lists and community-surfaced essays.
